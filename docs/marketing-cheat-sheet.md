# Changing the website with Claude — cheat sheet for the marketing team

## One-time setup (per person)

1. Get invited to the GitHub repo `alexcondrea/trustworkx-website`
   (ask Alexandru) and create/log into your GitHub account.
2. Install the **Claude** app on your phone (or use claude.ai in a browser)
   and log in with your team account.
3. In the Claude app, open the **Code** tab and connect your GitHub account
   when asked. Select the `trustworkx-website` repository.

## Making a change

1. Open the Claude app → Code → `trustworkx-website`.
2. Describe what you want, like you'd tell a colleague:
   - *"Change the headline on the homepage to 'Trust, built in.'"*
   - *"Make the contact button bigger and blue."*
   - *"Add a section about our Berlin office under the team section."*
3. Claude makes the change and replies with a preview link:
   **https://www-staging.trustworkx.de/**
   (username `marketing` — password: ask Alexandru; your phone remembers it
   after the first time)
4. Open the preview and check it. It's the real site, just not public.
5. Not right yet? Tell Claude what to adjust — the preview updates
   automatically (give it ~1 minute).
6. Happy? Reply **"publish"**. Claude puts it on the live site:
   **https://www.trustworkx.de/** (live within ~1 minute)

## Good to know

- Nothing goes live until you say "publish" — previews are harmless.
- Every change is saved in history and can be undone: just tell Claude
  *"undo the change from earlier"*.
- One preview site is shared by the whole team. If two people preview
  changes at the same moment, the later one wins — coordinate briefly if
  you're both editing.
- Claude won't add heavy frameworks or redesign the tech — the site stays
  simple and fast by design.
